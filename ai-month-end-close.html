What is an AI month-end close copilot?

It's a checklist and assistant that works inside your accounting tools. Each month it gathers bank and card activity, lines it up with the books, lists what's missing and drafts short explanations of big changes. A bookkeeper or CPA reviews and closes the month.

Month-end close without the scramble. The copilot runs your checklist, prepares reconciliations, chases missing receipts and drafts notes on what changed, so your bookkeeper reviews instead of hunting.

Who it's for

Businesses that close their books every month, and the bookkeepers or CPAs who run the close.

What it connects to

QuickBooks Online or Xero, bank and card feeds, Google Drive, email, a close checklist in Sheets or your task tool.

What the workflow can look like

  1. On the first business day, the close checklist opens with owners and due dates.
  2. AI pulls bank and card activity and lines it up against QuickBooks Online or Xero.
  3. Unmatched items and missing receipts go on a list, with draft requests to the right people.
  4. AI compares this month with last month and drafts notes on anything that moved a lot.
  5. Your bookkeeper reviews, adjusts and marks the month closed, and the owner gets a one-page summary.

Bank and card reconciliation preparation

Reconciliation means explaining the difference between your statement and your accounting record—not just finding similar amounts. A proposed review-first workflow can assemble a statement-to-ledger worksheet for each bank or card account, with the original evidence and a separate unresolved-items list.

  1. Start with owner-authorized statement or CSV exports and an agreed ledger period. Record opening/closing balances, account identifiers and the source date; do not assume a live bank-feed API is available.
  2. Prepare candidate matches using date, amount and reference. Separate transfers, card payments, fees, deposits in transit and outstanding checks so equal amounts do not become false matches.
  3. Flag missing receipts, possible duplicates and timing differences. Missing statement lines remain unresolved; AI never fills them in from guesses.
  4. Your bookkeeper checks the worksheet, approves every proposed accounting entry before posting and completes reconciliation inside the accounting system. A CPA reviews tax-sensitive items.

Access boundary: QuickBooks and Xero accounting APIs are not blanket access to raw bank-feed lines or an API right to finalize reconciliation. We verify your platform’s current supported endpoints and use approved exports when needed. Bank connections, statement availability and any third-party provider fees are scoped separately. No autonomous payments, transfers or filing.

Status: This specific worksheet design is a custom-build possibility, not a claimed tested integration or live customer system. The existing video is an illustrative sample, not proof of a connected bank feed.

Can matching replace my bookkeeper’s sign-off?

No. Suggested matches are working papers only. Your bookkeeper resolves exceptions and signs off in the accounting system; a match does not grant posting or payment permission.

What if my bank does not offer an API?

We assess authorized statement exports first. If neither statements nor supported data access are available, we cannot promise a complete reconciliation workflow.
