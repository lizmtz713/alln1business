How the connected flow works

  1. Returning clients get an organizer based on what they sent last year.
  2. Uploads go to your approved secure portal, not ordinary web forms or chat.
  3. A missing-item list and a reminder draft are prepared for staff review.
  4. A readiness queue shows which files are complete enough to start.
  5. Approved status answers handle routine “did you get it?” questions.
  6. The preparer verifies amounts and deadlines, prepares and files the return.

Illustrative design only. Exact steps, tools and approvals are agreed after your audit.

Questions before you start

Is this the same as the Year-End Tax Organizer page?

No. That page is written for small businesses working with an outside CPA. This page is for tax firms, which need separate client files, scale testing and confirmed portal and tax-software access before rollout.

Will AI prepare or file returns?

No. AI organizes documents and drafts reminders. The authorized preparer prepares, signs and files every return.

Where do clients upload sensitive documents?

To your firm’s approved secure portal. Website forms and chat are only for contact details and general questions.

Can AI read IRS notices?

It can summarize a notice and pull out the printed deadline for a preparer to confirm. AI can misread dates and amounts, so the preparer verifies every one.

When should we set this up?

Before the season is best, so the organizer and readiness queue are tested on a small client group first.
