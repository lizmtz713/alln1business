Questions before you start

Do my stylists have to learn a new system?

No. We set the reminders, waitlist, deposits and rebooks up in the booking tool you already use. The front desk approves the wording.

What if a client is upset about a fee or a text?

A person can take over. Fees only follow the policy you set and showed at booking. Texts go out only after carrier approval, and they are not sending from this website today.

Will it sound like a chain salon?

No. You approve the words. It should sound like your front desk.

Is my client list sold or shared?

No. We connect only the booking and payment accounts you grant. You can take that access away. We do not sell your client list.
