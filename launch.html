The honest fine print

Filings, fees & banking

  • Liz guides you and prepares the filing; you review and sign. Government filing fees are paid by you directly to the county or state (they vary by location).
  • For an LLC, we help with the state filing and recommend a licensed professional (attorney or CPA) where your situation needs one.
  • Your bank account is opened by you, at the bank you choose. We prepare the documents and checklist so the appointment goes smoothly. We never hold your money or accounts.
  • Not legal, tax or CPA advice. We don’t file taxes, choose your entity for you, or guarantee approvals. No grants or funding applications.
  • Third-party software (domain, email, hosting, CRM, phone) is billed by those vendors to you, on accounts in your name.
