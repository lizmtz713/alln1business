Can AI explain my financial statements?

Yes. AI can read your profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow statement and write a short summary in everyday words, in English or Spanish. It points out changes worth a question, and your accountant answers the questions that need judgment.

Financial statements, explained the way a person would. Each month you get a short letter about your numbers: what went up, what went down, why it matters and what to ask your accountant.

Who it's for

Owners who get financial statements but don't have time to decode them.

What it connects to

QuickBooks Online or Xero reports, email.

What the workflow can look like

  1. After the month closes, reports are pulled from QuickBooks Online or Xero.
  2. AI compares the month with last month and the same month last year.
  3. It writes a one-page letter covering sales, margins, big expenses, cash and what you owe.
  4. It lists three questions worth asking your bookkeeper or CPA.
  5. The letter arrives by email, and you can reply with follow-up questions.

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

Questions before you start

Is this financial advice?

No. It explains your own numbers in plain words and suggests questions. Advice comes from your CPA or financial advisor.

Can it be in Spanish?

Yes. Letters can be in English, Spanish or both.

What if my books aren't up to date?

The letter is only as good as the books. If they're behind, we recommend catching up first.

Can my partners or managers get it too?

Yes. You choose who receives it.
