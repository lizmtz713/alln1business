What does an AI collections assistant do?

It watches your open invoices and drafts the right follow-up at the right time: a friendly reminder before the due date, a firmer note after it, and a call list for accounts that need a person. Every message waits for your OK unless you approve a template ahead of time.

Get paid without the awkward chasing. The assistant watches unpaid invoices, drafts polite reminders in your voice, logs promises to pay and gives you a short call list each week.

Who it's for

Service businesses, contractors, agencies and distributors that invoice on terms.

What it connects to

QuickBooks Online, Xero or your invoicing tool, Stripe or your payment link, Gmail or Outlook.

What the workflow can look like

  1. Open invoices sync from QuickBooks Online, Xero or your invoicing tool.
  2. Each invoice gets a reminder schedule based on your terms and the customer's history.
  3. AI drafts each reminder in English or Spanish with the invoice and payment link attached.
  4. Replies like "paying Friday" are logged as promises, with a check on the promised date.
  5. Each week you get a short list: who paid, who promised and who to call.

Questions before you start

Will reminders sound robotic?

No. They're written in your tone, short and polite, and you can edit any draft before it goes.

Can it send reminders by text?

Email is the default. Texting needs the customer's consent and a registered business texting number, so we set it up only where both are in place.

Is this a collection agency?

No. It helps you follow up on your own invoices. It doesn't collect debts for you or pressure customers.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. Reminders can go out in English or Spanish based on each customer's preference.
