The problem

A product or part you sold is recalled, and the customers who bought it are scattered across old orders.

What it does

  1. You enter the recall facts: the item, the date range, and the notice wording.
  2. Match those orders in the sales records you already keep.
  3. Draft a customer notice from those facts, without adding claims that are not in the notice you provided.
  4. A person approves the list and the wording.
  5. Approved notices go by email. Text only if the customer agreed and texting is approved.

Illustrative steps only. This is not a client story or a promise of a result. Exact scope is agreed after the audit.

Who it's for

Shops, distributors, and installers who need to notify past buyers of a product or part recall.

What it connects to

The CRM, store, or order history you already use; email; a texting tool once carrier approval is in place.
