The problem

Certified payroll for public jobs is assembled by hand from timesheets, classifications, and the wage rates in the contract.

What it does

  1. Pull the week's timecards and payroll register.
  2. Split hours by project, worker, day, and labor classification.
  3. Compare hourly rates and fringe with the wage determination in the contract.
  4. Draft the weekly report in the format that project uses, with mismatches flagged.
  5. An authorized person reviews every line before anyone signs or submits.

Illustrative steps only. This is not a client story or a promise of a result. Exact scope is agreed after the audit.

Who it's for

Government contractors who file certified payroll on public jobs.

What it connects to

Gusto, ADP, or QuickBooks Payroll, where each one allows access; timecard apps and spreadsheets; wage-determination PDFs; agency report forms.
