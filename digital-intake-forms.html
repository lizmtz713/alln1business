The problem

Intake is still a paper or PDF form, and someone retypes the answers into the CRM.

What it does

  1. Turn the form you already use into an online form.
  2. Send the link when an appointment or an inquiry is created.
  3. Flag missing required answers and missing signatures.
  4. Put the finished answers into the CRM.
  5. A person reviews them before they are treated as the record.
