The starting price covers Alln1’s scoped setup work. Third-party subscriptions and usage charges are billed by the vendors to your own accounts; the audit estimates those costs.

Will AI act without my approval?

We define permissions and approval points before launch. AI is identified as AI. Quotes, sensitive decisions and public content need the agreed human review.
