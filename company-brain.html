What is a company knowledge AI assistant?

It's a private assistant that works from your business's own documents. Staff ask questions in plain words, and it answers from your procedures and records with a link to the source. If the answer isn't in your documents, it says so and sends the question to a person.

Who it's for

Owner-run teams of 3 to 50 people, field service companies, clinics and offices with lots of procedures.

What it connects to

Google Drive or SharePoint documents, manuals and PDFs, recorded walkthroughs, Slack, Teams or website chat.

What the workflow can look like

  1. Your procedures, manuals, price lists and FAQs are gathered into one private library.
  2. Owners record short walkthroughs of how things are done, and AI turns them into written steps.
  3. Staff ask questions by chat, in English or Spanish.
  4. Answers cite the document they came from, and unknown questions go to a manager.
  5. A monthly report shows the most-asked questions and the gaps to fill.

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

What stays in your hands

  • You choose which documents it can see.
  • It answers only from your material and never guesses on prices or policies.
  • You approve any new procedure before it's added.

Questions before you start

Is my data used to train public AI models?

We set it up with business tools and settings whose terms say your data isn't used to train their models, and we document which tools see what.

Can it help with equipment troubleshooting?

Yes. Load your manuals and service notes, and techs can ask about error codes and fixes from the field.

What happens when it doesn't know?

It says it doesn't know and sends the question to a person you choose.

Can new hires use it for training?

Yes. It's a good first stop for new staff, alongside the person training them.
