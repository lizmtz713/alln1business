Solo attorneys and small firms with steady inbound inquiries and a repeatable consultation process, where phone tag and admin work compete with client matters. New firms can start with the launch stage; busy practices usually start with the inquiry-to-consultation pipeline.

Fictional example: a two-attorney family law practice

Picture an imaginary two-attorney family law practice. A prospective client fills in the website form on a Tuesday afternoon while both attorneys are in hearings. The AI replies, says it is AI, collects only name, contact details and the general practice area, and does not ask for case facts. The firm runs its conflict check; once an attorney clears it, the prospect gets consultation times and a reminder the day before. The attorney decides whether to take the matter and approves the engagement letter.

Made up to show how the pieces connect. Not a real business, client story, testimonial or measured result.

How the connected flow works

  1. The AI identifies itself and collects only minimal contact and practice-area details.
  2. The firm runs its conflict check; no case facts are requested before that.
  3. Once cleared, the prospect gets authorized consultation times.
  4. Reminders reduce no-shows; meeting notes become tasks.
  5. The attorney decides on representation and approves the engagement letter.
  6. Routine invoice reminders follow ordinary approved billing only, not trust accounting.

Illustrative design only. Exact steps, tools and approvals are agreed after your audit.

Questions before you start

Will the AI give legal advice?

No. It identifies itself as AI, collects contact details and schedules consultations under your rules. Legal questions go to an attorney.

How are conflicts handled?

The firm runs its own conflict check. Intake collects only minimal details first, and consultation times are offered after a person clears the conflict.

Is client information kept confidential?

Confidentiality is a design requirement: minimal data, access controls, vendor review and no-training terms where available. The responsible attorney approves the setup.

Does it connect to my case-management software?

We check your specific tool and its API access in the audit. We don’t claim support for a legal software integration until it has been verified.

Does it work for any practice area?

Inquiry and scheduling workflows suit most consumer and small-business practices. Each firm’s ethics rules and intake limits are reviewed before launch.
