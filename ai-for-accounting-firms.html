Who this package is for

Owner-led bookkeeping and accounting practices with recurring monthly clients, usually on QuickBooks Online Accountant or Xero, where skilled staff lose hours collecting and re-keying inputs instead of reviewing them. Sole practitioners opening a new firm can start at the first stage; established firms usually start with a pilot on a handful of clients.

Problems we hear in this industry

Clients go quiet when you ask for documents

Statements, receipts and answers arrive late, in pieces and in different places, so the close waits on the slowest client.

The same data gets typed more than once

Information moves between email, portals, spreadsheets and the ledger by hand, and every hand-off is a chance for a mistake.

Nobody can see which clients are ready to close

Readiness lives in people’s heads and inboxes rather than one list the whole team can trust.

Staff answer the same questions every month

“Did you get my statement?” and “What do you still need?” interrupt review time.

Collections and explanations eat partner time

Chasing unpaid invoices and explaining reports to clients competes with billable work.

Fictional example · not a client

Fictional example: a five-person bookkeeping firm

Picture an imaginary five-person firm with 60 monthly clients. On the first business day, each client gets a checklist of what is still missing. Uploads and emailed statements are named and filed to the right client folder; anything unreadable or unclear lands in an exception queue. Suggested categories wait for a bookkeeper’s approval, and a readiness board shows the partner which twelve clients can close today and which eight are still waiting. Client reminder drafts go out only after staff review them.

Made up to show how the pieces connect. Not a real business, client story, testimonial or measured result.

How the connected flow works

  1. A scheduled checklist goes to each client for the month.
  2. Uploads and emails are matched to the right client and filed.
  3. A missing-item request is drafted for staff to review and send.
  4. Transactions get suggested categories; unclear items become exceptions.
  5. A readiness dashboard shows which clients are ready to close.
  6. A bookkeeper approves entries and the client message before anything posts or sends.

Illustrative design only. Exact steps, tools and approvals are agreed after your audit.

Questions before you start

How is this different from the Bookkeeping Firm Automation Pack?

The Pack is one system: document collection, transaction prep and close checklists. This page is the full roadmap for a firm, from launch to established, and uses the Pack as the core of the growing stage.

Does it work with QuickBooks Online Accountant and Xero?

Those are the most common ledgers we scope for. Exact API access, permissions and any developer or connector fees are confirmed in the audit before we propose a build.

Will AI post entries to client books on its own?

No. AI prepares suggestions in a review queue. A human approves each entry before anything posts, including recurring items.

How is each client’s data kept separate?

Access is set client by client and role by role, the data stays in your firm’s own accounts, and we document which tool sees what.

Can we start with a few clients?

Yes. A pilot with a handful of clients is the usual start, so you can compare against your own baseline before expanding.
