The problem

A load needs mill test reports that match the purchase order and the job spec, and a missing heat number holds the truck.

What it does

  1. Read incoming reports for heat number, grade, specification, and supplier.
  2. File each report with its purchase order and receiving record.
  3. When an order is picked, match every line to a report and to the job spec.
  4. Flag missing or mismatched lines.
  5. Quality staff accept or reject. Shipping waits on that decision.

Illustrative steps only. This is not a client story or a promise of a result. Exact scope is agreed after the audit.

Who it's for

Manufacturers and fabricators who ship material that needs mill test reports.

What it connects to

Prophet 21, NetSuite, or Epicor, where each one allows access; Google Drive or SharePoint; supplier email; packing lists.
