What is a 13-week cash flow forecast?

It's a week-by-week view of the cash you expect to come in and go out over the next three months. Lenders and advisors use it because it shows the tight weeks early. AI keeps it current from your books, open invoices and upcoming bills, and explains what changed.

Who it's for

Owners with payroll, seasonal swings or a line of credit, and the CPAs who advise them.

What it connects to

Your bank balance, QuickBooks Online or Xero, open invoices and bills, your payroll schedule, Google Sheets.

What the workflow can look like

  1. Starting cash comes from your bank balance and books.
  2. Money coming in comes from open invoices and your customers' usual payment timing.
  3. Money going out comes from bills, payroll, rent, loan payments and recurring costs.
  4. Each week the forecast rolls forward and compares what happened with what was expected.
  5. You get a short note: the lowest-cash week ahead, why, and options to consider.

Questions before you start

How accurate is the forecast?

It's as good as the data and assumptions behind it. It improves as it compares forecast with actual each week, and it says when a number is an estimate.

Why 13 weeks?

Thirteen weeks is one quarter. It's long enough to see trouble coming and short enough to plan week by week.

Can I share it with my lender?

Yes. It can produce a clean version for a lender or partner, which you review before sharing.

Do I need QuickBooks?

No. It can work from QuickBooks Online, Xero or spreadsheets.
