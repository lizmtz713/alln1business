FAQ

Questions, answered

Why not just use ChatGPT myself?

Use it! It’s great for writing and ideas. But a chatbot only answers when you type. We build systems that act on their own across your phone, CRM, calendar, email, accounting and website — with memory, handoffs between agents, and approval steps — then we monitor them.

What exactly do you connect?

The tools you already run on: CRM (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce), phone/SMS, calendars, Gmail/Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Stripe/Square, Google Sheets/Airtable, Shopify/WordPress, Google Business Profile, social and review platforms, and most apps with an API. We use Make, Zapier, n8n, native integrations and direct APIs.

Do I have to switch software?

Usually no. We build around what you already use and only recommend a new tool when it clearly pays for itself. Everything is built in your own accounts, so you own it.

Who pays for the software?

You do, directly to each vendor (CRM, phone numbers, AI usage, Make, etc.). Our setup price covers our work — flat and all-inclusive, no nickel-and-diming.

Are Aria, Rex, Orbit, Sage and Pulse real people?

No. They are AI agents, labeled AI everywhere. Liz Martinez is the human founder who architects and reviews every system. You approve anything customer-facing.

Will AI send things without my OK?

Only what you’ve approved it to send. Quotes, refunds, complaints, and anything sensitive are routed to a human for a yes before they go out.

What happens when an app changes or something breaks?

That’s what Managed AI Ops is for: monitoring, error alerts, fixes and monthly tuning, from $149/mo.

Do you guarantee results or rankings?

No. We don’t promise rankings or revenue, and we don’t publish fake case studies. Example flows on this site are example scenarios.

Where do you work?

Online, nationwide. Based in Houston, with in-person meetings by appointment in Greater Houston. No street address is published.

Can you help me start a business from scratch?

Yes. AI-Ready Business Launch takes you from idea to open: we prepare your DBA/assumed name (or help with an LLC state filing), walk you through the EIN, prep your bank account paperwork, and build your brand, website, email, Google Business Profile and social, connected with AI from day one.
