Why not just use ChatGPT?

AI assistants can already use connectors, memory and scheduled actions. We set that up around your business, test it, and support it for you, including access, connections, and human-review rules. Optional Managed AI Ops covers agreed maintenance after launch.

What exactly do you connect?

The tools you already run on: CRM (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce), phone/SMS, calendars, Gmail/Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Stripe/Square, Google Sheets/Airtable, Shopify/WordPress, Google Business Profile, social and review platforms, and most apps with an API. We set up those connections, test them, and support them for you.

Do I have to switch software?

Usually no. We build around what you already use and only recommend a new tool when it clearly pays for itself. We set it up in your own accounts, so you own it.

Who pays for the software?

You do, directly to each vendor (CRM, phone numbers, AI usage, and the other tools we connect). Our setup price covers our work — flat and all-inclusive, no nickel-and-diming.

Are Aria, Rex, Orbit, Sage and Pulse real people?

No. They are AI agents, labeled AI everywhere. Liz is the human founder and reviews every system we set up, test and support. You approve anything customer-facing.

Will AI send things without my OK?

Only what you’ve approved it to send. Quotes, refunds, complaints, and anything sensitive are routed to a human for a yes before they go out.

What happens when an app changes or something breaks?

That’s what Managed AI Ops is for: we watch it, fix what breaks, and tune it, from $149/mo.

Do you guarantee results or rankings?

No. We don’t promise rankings or revenue, and we don’t publish fake case studies. Example flows on this site are example scenarios.

Where do you work?

Online, nationwide. Based in Houston, with in-person meetings by appointment in Greater Houston.
