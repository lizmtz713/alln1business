Who this is for

For home-service owners whose office retypes customer details, hunts for job photos or chases incomplete closeout notes. Jobber and Housecall Pro are alternative source systems here; you do not need both.

Problems worth solving

The office cannot quote from an incomplete handoff

A customer description, site photos and technician notes often arrive separately. A scoped workflow can assemble a job brief, identify missing information and prepare a quote draft using your approved service list.

Finished work is not always ready for billing

A completed visit may still need a signed change order or a materials breakdown. An exception checklist can show the office what is missing before it reviews the invoice. It does not certify work quality or approve charges.

What a custom workflow could look like

  1. An authorized job or client event enters the workflow, or staff provide an approved export if the needed event is unavailable.
  2. The system checks the source record identifier before matching the customer; uncertain matches stop for review.
  3. AI summarizes authorized notes and document references into an office handoff, with missing fields listed explicitly.
  4. The owner reviews any quote, customer message or accounting entry before it is sent or posted. Dispatch changes remain with the team.
  5. The scoped integration records the reviewed outcome where supported and logs errors for staff instead of silently retrying charges or creating duplicate jobs.

Illustrative design only—not a client story, measured result or demonstration of a live integration.

Access and plan limitations

Jobber uses a GraphQL API and OAuth; private custom integrations are for specific approved customers/developers. Draft apps are limited to five paying accounts without additional approval. Its documentation does not establish a universal subscription-plan entitlement, so we confirm your eligibility with Jobber. Housecall Pro’s public API requires MAX and an admin-generated key. Not every in-app feature is exposed; required fields, events and read/write permissions must be checked before promising a connection.

Questions before you start

Will it work on my current plan?

Not assumed. Housecall Pro requires MAX for public API access. Jobber custom integration eligibility must be confirmed for your account; we do not promise access based on a plan name alone.

Can AI dispatch crews or change customer prices?

Not in this proposed workflow. Staff retain dispatch and pricing decisions, and review quotes and messages before release.

Is this the same as Photo-to-Quote?

No. Photo-to-Quote focuses on preparing a quote from submitted images. This page concerns the broader field-service record and office handoff, subject to what your vendor exposes.

Can you connect the job to bookkeeping?

Potentially, after checking both systems and the mapping. A human reviews accounting entries before posting. No autonomous payments or tax filing are included.
