What the workflow can look like

  1. An invoice or receipt is received in the agreed folder or inbox
  2. AI extracts vendor, amount, due date and category for review
  3. The original file is linked to the extracted record
  4. A draft bill or task is prepared in the connected tool
  5. You verify details before posting or paying

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

What stays in your hands

No automatic payments. You or your bookkeeper check the original, duplicates, categories and amounts. This is bookkeeping preparation, not tax or CPA advice.
