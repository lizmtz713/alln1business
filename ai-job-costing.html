Know which jobs actually made money. AI pulls labor, materials, subs and change orders into each job's cost, compares it with the estimate and flags jobs drifting over budget while there's still time to act.

Who it's for

Contractors, trades, installers and agencies that sell by the job or project.

What it connects to

QuickBooks Online, Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan, timesheets, supplier bills, Google Sheets.

What the workflow can look like

  1. Estimates and jobs come in from your field software, QuickBooks Online or spreadsheets.
  2. Hours, receipts, supplier bills and subcontractor invoices are matched to jobs.
  3. Costs that don't match a job are listed for someone to assign.
  4. Each job's actual cost is compared with its estimate, including change orders and extras.
  5. A weekly report ranks jobs by margin and explains the biggest misses.

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

Questions before you start

Which businesses is this for?

Contractors, trades, installers, agencies and any business that sells by the job or project.

Does it handle work-in-progress (WIP) reports?

It can prepare WIP schedules from job data for your accountant to review. Your accountant signs off on the final numbers.

Will it find work we forgot to bill?

It flags change orders and extras that show up in job records but not on invoices, so you can decide whether to bill them.

What software does it work with?

QuickBooks Online and common field tools such as Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan, depending on what each allows. We confirm your setup in the audit.
