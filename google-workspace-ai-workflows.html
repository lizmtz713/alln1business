Who this is for

For owners and office teams who use Google Workspace but still copy information between email, folders and spreadsheets. The design starts with one useful workflow, not domain-wide access to everything.

Problems worth solving

The spreadsheet is missing the evidence

A row saying “invoice received” is not enough if nobody can find the PDF. We can prepare a record that includes the source file, extracted fields and unresolved questions, subject to approved access.

A shared folder can become an accidental data leak

The workflow should preserve permissions rather than make every document broadly visible. We define who can read the source, who sees the AI output and when data is deleted before implementation.

What a custom workflow could look like

  1. A user-authorized Gmail label or approved Drive folder defines intake; unrelated mail and files stay out of scope.
  2. The workflow records message/file IDs and checks whether an item already has a review record.
  3. AI extracts agreed fields or summarizes the request into a proposed Sheets row or task, with links to permitted sources.
  4. Missing fields and ambiguous instructions are flagged. The owner reviews external replies, sharing changes and accounting entries before they take effect.
  5. The approved outcome is logged, and quota or permission failures surface in an exception queue rather than disappearing.

Illustrative design only—not a client story, measured result or demonstration of a live integration.

Access and plan limitations

Gmail, Drive and Sheets are separate APIs with OAuth scopes and quotas; a Workspace or Gemini subscription does not automatically authorize them. Gmail read/compose scopes can be restricted: external-app verification and, when applicable, a security assessment for transmitted or stored restricted data may be required. Internal-use exceptions must be checked against Google’s rules, not assumed. Domain-wide delegation requires a Workspace super administrator and is not the default. Admin app controls, shared-drive access and edition-specific features must be confirmed.

Questions before you start

Does Gemini for Workspace automatically connect all my tools?

No. Gemini product features, API usage and authorization are separate questions. We verify the exact workflow and licenses instead of promising a universal connection.

Can you bypass Google app verification?

No. We use a compliant access path, check any applicable internal-use exception and allow for verification or security review where required.

Will this change Drive sharing permissions?

Not without human approval. The design uses existing authorized access and keeps source links subject to their permissions.

Can the workflow prepare bookkeeping data?

It can be scoped to prepare reviewable inputs. A human approves every accounting entry before posting; a CPA reviews tax items. No autonomous payments or filing are included.
