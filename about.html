Meet Liz

I learned how businesses really run from the inside.

I’m Liz. I started Alln1 Business in 2017. Before AI became part of the work, I spent years inside the day-to-day of running businesses: the orders, the books, the schedules and the people.

For more than 14 years (2010–2024) I was Corporate Manager at Industrial Surplus Inc. I managed corporate and Florida branch operations and led a team of 10. I worked across accounting, HR, procurement and online sales on Amazon and eBay, and I ran the financial reporting in QuickBooks: P&L statements, accounts payable and accounts receivable.

I also worked as an accounting associate. At Texas Injection Molding (2015–2017) I started as production manager and moved through scheduling and planning, shipping and receiving, customer service, accounting and HR. At Chempak International (2017–2018) I worked on payables, receivables and filing.

In 2021 I took the Rice University FinTech Boot Camp (Financial Technologies). I hold a BS in Political Science & Government with a double major in Psychology.
