Who this is for

For service businesses and bookkeeping teams using Xero who lose time connecting supplier emails, document folders and the ledger. This is a Xero-specific integration design, not a replacement for your accountant or Xero’s own features.

Problems worth solving

The invoice and the explanation live in different places

A supplier email can explain a deposit, credit or revised invoice while the accounting record shows only a total. We can link source evidence to a review item so the person making the decision sees the context.

Unclear inputs slow down the whole queue

Missing tax details, an unfamiliar supplier or an unclear expense category should become an exception, not an AI guess that silently reaches the ledger. Suggested coding stays visible beside the original document.

What a custom workflow could look like

  1. An invoice arrives in an approved mailbox or folder; the workflow records its source and checks whether that file was already handled.
  2. AI extracts dates, amounts and supplier details into a staging queue, checks arithmetic and suggests an existing contact and account.
  3. Low-confidence fields, duplicate references and missing evidence are flagged. The reviewer can correct or reject the suggestion.
  4. A human approves each accounting entry before any posting. The scoped build determines which approved records the API can create and which steps stay inside Xero.
  5. A review log records the source, approver, resulting record identifier and any failed attempt, so retries do not create duplicate entries.

Illustrative design only—not a client story, measured result or demonstration of a live integration.

Access and plan limitations

Xero uses OAuth authorization for a specific organization and scoped access. New apps use granular Accounting API scopes; read-only variants can support review-first work. Developer connection tiers, usage fees and endpoint restrictions are separate from your Xero subscription. Journals access is restricted; we do not assume it is included. Accounting API bank transactions are not a promise of access to unreconciled bank-feed lines or automated reconciliation.

Questions before you start

Does AI change my Xero books without review?

No. AI prepares suggestions in a staging queue. A human approves each entry before posting; this page grants no recurring auto-posting permission.

Is Xero API access included with your setup?

Your Xero account and any developer-tier or connector fees remain separate. We check current connection limits, permissions and endpoint access during the audit before proposing the build.

Can you automatically reconcile bank feeds?

That is not offered here. We can scope reconciliation preparation from authorized exports and accounting data; your bookkeeper completes and approves reconciliation in the accounting system.

Will this replace my current accountant?

No. Your bookkeeper reviews accounting work, and a CPA reviews tax items. The purpose is to prepare traceable inputs, not provide accounting or tax advice.
