What does an AI tax organizer do?

It builds a checklist of the documents your CPA needs, collects them from email and folders, flags what's missing and tracks W-9s and 1099 details for contractors. When a tax notice arrives, it summarizes what the notice says, the deadline and what to send your CPA.

Who it's for

What it connects to

Gmail or Outlook, Google Drive, QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar.

What the workflow can look like

  1. In the fall, a checklist is built from last year's return and this year's activity.
  2. AI collects statements, 1099s, W-9s and receipts into one organized folder.
  3. Missing items go on a list, with draft requests for you to send.
  4. When a notice from the IRS or the Texas Comptroller arrives, AI summarizes it and puts the response deadline on your calendar.
  5. Your CPA gets the folder and the notice summary, ready to review.

Questions before you start

Is this tax advice?

No. It organizes documents and explains notices in plain words. Your CPA or tax professional gives the advice and files.

Can it help with 1099s?

It collects W-9s and tracks contractor payments so your CPA or payroll provider can prepare 1099s.

What should I do when I get an IRS notice?

Don't ignore it, and note the deadline. Send it to your tax professional, and the notice reader gives them a summary to start from.

Does it handle Texas sales tax notices?

It can summarize Texas Comptroller notices and track their deadlines. Your tax professional handles the response.
