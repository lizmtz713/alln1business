Who this is for

For owners and bookkeeping firms inheriting an overgrown QuickBooks Online or Xero chart. The goal is a cleaner proposal your bookkeeper can evaluate, with reporting continuity protected before any change is made.

Problems worth solving

Similar account names hide different treatment

AI can group similarly named accounts and show examples of their use. Similarity is not proof they should merge: tax treatment, subaccounts, reporting and historical balances require professional review.

Changing the chart can change the story

Renaming or reclassifying an account can affect comparisons, integrations and the reports owners rely on. We prepare an impact checklist so the reviewer sees dependencies before approving a change.

What a custom workflow could look like

  1. Take an authorized read-only export of the chart and agreed transaction samples, preserving account identifiers and original names.
  2. Identify similar labels, inactive candidates and inconsistent use; distinguish observations from recommended actions.
  3. Prepare a proposed map with reasons, sample evidence and unresolved questions for each suggested change.
  4. Your bookkeeper approves or rejects each recommendation; a CPA reviews tax-sensitive classifications and any implications.
  5. Approved changes are implemented only through a separately agreed process. This proposed preparation workflow performs no automatic merges, deletions, reclassifications or posting.

Illustrative design only—not a client story, measured result or demonstration of a live integration.

Access and plan limitations

QuickBooks Online and Xero expose account resources through their Accounting APIs, subject to company authorization and scopes. Account types, active status, transaction history and supported changes have platform-specific constraints. Read access does not mean that merging, deleting or rewriting history is supported by API or permitted in your company. We can begin from approved exports, and confirm exact endpoint permissions before proposing any later write access.

Questions before you start

Will AI merge accounts for me?

No. This is cleanup preparation. It proposes a map and flags questions; it does not automatically merge, delete or rewrite historical entries.

Does this replace bookkeeping cleanup?

No. It helps organize the evidence and decisions. Your bookkeeper is responsible for review and any approved accounting changes.

Can this improve tax categories?

It can flag categories for discussion, but it does not decide tax treatment. A CPA reviews tax items before any change is approved.

Do we need live API access to start?

Not always. An authorized chart export and agreed samples can support the initial review. API permissions, plan features and supported operations are confirmed in the audit.
