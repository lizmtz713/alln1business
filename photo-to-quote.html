Can AI create a quote from photos?

AI can read photos to identify the item, its condition and rough size, then build a draft quote from your price list and rules. It flags what it can't tell from a photo, such as hidden damage or exact measurements, so you decide whether a site visit is needed.

Who it's for

Roofing, fencing, junk removal, landscaping, cleaning, auto body and repair trades.

What it connects to

A website upload form or chat, your price list in Sheets, your CRM or quoting tool, email.

What the workflow can look like

  1. A customer uploads photos through your website or chat, or a crew sends them from the job.
  2. AI identifies what's in the photos and asks for anything missing, like a measurement or a wider shot.
  3. It builds a draft quote from your price list, with options and notes on what it couldn't confirm.
  4. You review, adjust and approve the quote.
  5. The approved quote goes to the customer, and follow-ups are scheduled.

Questions before you start

What kinds of businesses use photo quoting?

Roofing, fencing, junk removal, landscaping, cleaning, auto body, appliance repair and other trades that can see the job in a picture.

How accurate are photo estimates?

Good enough for a draft. Accuracy depends on the photos and the job, and anything uncertain is flagged for you.

Can customers send photos by text?

Yes, where customers have agreed to texts and your business texting number is registered. A web upload works for everyone.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. Customers and crews can describe the job in Spanish or English.
