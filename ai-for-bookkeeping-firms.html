Firms use it to chase client documents, read statements and receipts, suggest categories, run each client's close checklist and draft answers to routine client questions. The firm keeps its review steps and its client relationships, and the repetitive prep is done before anyone opens the file.

Who it's for

Bookkeeping and accounting firms with many small-business clients.

What it connects to

QuickBooks Online Accountant, Xero, client email or portals, Google Drive, a firm dashboard in Sheets.

What the workflow can look like

  1. Each client gets a monthly checklist and a document request schedule.
  2. Client uploads and emails are read, named and filed to the right client folder.
  3. Transactions get suggested categories, and anything unclear becomes a question for the client.
  4. A firm dashboard shows which clients are ready to close and which are waiting on documents.
  5. Routine client questions get draft answers for staff to review and send.

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

Questions before you start

Will AI replace my staff?

It takes over repetitive prep, not judgment. Firms usually use the time for more clients or advisory work.

Does it work with QuickBooks Online Accountant and Xero?

Yes, those are the most common. Other practice tools are reviewed in the audit.

How is client data protected?

Data stays in your firm's own accounts, access is limited to what each step needs, and we document which tools see what.

Can we start with a few clients?

Yes. A pilot with a handful of clients is the usual start.
