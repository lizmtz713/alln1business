What the workflow can look like

  1. Your records show a past customer who has gone quiet
  2. AI drafts a personal win-back offer from your real services
  3. You approve the message before it is sent
  4. A reply or booking is saved with that customer’s record
  5. Anyone who opts out is not contacted again

Illustrative scenario only. This is not a client case study, a live product demo or a promise of specific results.

What stays in your hands

AI drafts the offer. You approve anything that goes out. A call or text is sent only with that customer’s consent.

What we confirm in your AI Audit

  • Your source tools, account ownership and supported integration access
  • The information the AI may use and the data it must not access
  • Triggers, approval steps, exceptions and the person responsible for handoffs
  • A fixed build scope, third-party costs and how the workflow will be tested
