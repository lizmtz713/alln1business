Key facts

An AI phone receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your business line with only the facts you approved. It can take a message, offer a time you already opened, or ring your cell when the caller says a word you chose.

  • You keep the number on the truck and on Google. Callers do not learn a new one.
  • It says it is an assistant. It does not pretend to be your office manager.
  • English, Spanish, or both. You approve every sentence.
  • Nothing on this page is a live line. We set yours up after the audit and test it with you first.
  • A follow-up text is optional. Texting needs carrier approval and is not sending from this website.

What is going wrong while you are on the job?

The phone is the next job. You cannot hold it and the wrench at the same time. Here is that moment in your words, and what this receptionist can do about it.

You’re on the ladder and the phone is in the truck

How this fixes it. It answers on your number, in the greeting you approved.

They hang up instead of leaving a message

How this fixes it. They get questions, not a beep. Invoca’s 2024 data found under 3% of voicemail callers left a message.

You call back and they already hired the next company

How this fixes it. It can offer an opening you already published, or hold the details for your tap.

A real emergency is mixed in with “can you come Thursday”

How this fixes it. Transfer words you write, such as gas smell or water that will not stop, ring your cell.

The caller speaks Spanish and the office does not

How this fixes it. Spanish can be on, with words you approved. See bilingual AI.

You’re afraid it will invent a price

How this fixes it. It says only what you wrote. Anything else is “a person will confirm.”

What does a day look like with it answering?

One made-up Tuesday, by the clock. This is an example, not a client story, a testimonial, or a promise that your day will match it.

7:10 a.m. You are loading the van. A no-cool call is answered, offered a window you already opened, and waiting on your phone at the supply house.

2:40 p.m. You are in a crawlspace. “I smell gas” is on your transfer list, so your cell rings. Tune-up calls from that hour stay with the assistant.

6:15 p.m. You closed at 5. It says you are closed, takes the name and the problem, and does not promise a truck tonight. A text, if you want one, waits for carrier approval.

How does it work, step by step?

Six steps. You approve the words before any of them talk to a real customer.

  1. Your phone rings on the number already on the truck.
  2. It answers in your words and says it is an assistant. It does not search the web.
  3. It asks what you would ask: name, address, what is wrong, and when someone is home.
  4. It books, takes a message, or transfers, using the path you picked.
  5. Urgent words ring you. A gas smell comes through. A filter change does not, unless you say so.
  6. You get a recap with the name, number, address, and what happened. No new program.

What ways can we set it up?

It is not one rigid script. The audit picks the version that matches how you already answer. You can change a setting later.

  • English, Spanish, or both. It can switch when the caller does. You approve both.
  • After-hours wording. Message only, or the next open morning. No truck tonight unless you wrote that.
  • Transfer words. The list that rings your cell: gas, a leak that will not stop, water through a ceiling. A salon might add “allergic reaction.” A property manager might add “no water in the building.”
  • Approve-first, or automatic for low-risk steps. You tap yes before a booking, or it fills only openings you published. A price, a promise to roll a truck, and anything about a tenant stay with a person.
  • Book, gather a quote, or take a message. Booking needs real openings. It does not invent a price. Photo pricing is photo-to-quote.
  • Tools. Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Google Calendar, or your customer list. We connect to them. We are not their partner. The audit checks the login.

How does it look for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, a salon, and property management?

What do you actually see each day?

You do not learn a new dashboard. You see a recap after each call, by email or in the job software you already open: who called, how to reach them, what they need, and what the assistant did.

You see a tap when something is waiting. That is an approve-first booking, or a caller the assistant was not sure about. If you do not tap, the time is not booked. The caller was told a person would confirm.

Your cell rings only for the transfer list. If a call is recorded, the caller is told.

What stays in your hands, and what does it not do?

Nothing is said that you did not approve, unless you later choose automatic booking into openings you already published. You can turn the assistant off. You can take any one call. You can change the greeting, the hours, and the transfer list. You can revoke a tool you connected.

  • It does not pretend to be a human employee.
  • It does not invent a price, an arrival time, or a diagnosis.
  • It does not decide a tenant issue, a refund, or a legal notice. A person does.
  • It does not text until the carrier approves. Texting is not live on this website.
  • It is not a number you can call from this page today.
  • It does not replace you on a dangerous call. Those ring because you listed the words.
  • It does not promise every call is answered. If a forward fails, we want to hear that in testing, not from a customer.

Questions owners ask

Can I keep my number?

Yes. People keep dialing the number on the truck, the cards, and Google. We forward that number.

Can a real person still get the call?

Yes. Take every call, only the transfer words, or switch the assistant off. If you cannot pick up, it finishes the message path you approved.

How is this different from an answering service?

An answering service reads a script and sends a note. This uses your facts, your openings, and your transfer words. The table above also sets it next to voicemail and a hire.

What if it gets something wrong?

You hear mistakes in testing, before a customer does. After launch the recap shows what was said, and we correct the wording. It cannot offer a price you did not write.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. Stop the forward and calls come back to your phone or to voicemail. We show you that switch during setup.

Will it speak Spanish?

It can, only if you want it, and you approve those sentences. Other languages are on the more languages page.

Will customers know it is AI?

Yes. The greeting says it is an assistant. It will not pretend a person is in the office. The tone can still sound like your shop.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. You keep opening the email or Jobber you already check. We set this beside those tools.
