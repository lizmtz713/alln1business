## Rules held

- No catalog prices. The $497 AI Audit line is the only price on the new pages.
- No client stats or testimonials. Example days are labeled as examples.
- No “coming soon” lines.
- Copy says we connect to or work alongside software. It does not claim a vendor partnership.
- Restaurant phone ordering is a voice path that is set up for the restaurant. It is not a line you can call from the page.
- Handbook and shift pages say texting needs carrier approval and is not sending from the site. A desk message in Teams or Slack does not need that step. A person approves the policy or the schedule change.
- Safety talks are not a substitute for required OSHA training. The training coach does not issue a license or a certification.
- The safe-use policy is a draft. A lawyer reviews the final policy. It is not legal advice.
- Private AI says health information needs a signed agreement. It is not medical, legal, or tax advice. If the job is too specialized, the audit says pilot or referral.
- Cost, asset, lender, and multi-entity pages say a person reviews before a price, a schedule, a bank packet, or a combined view is treated as final. They do not promise a margin, a tax result, or that a covenant was met.
- Maintenance predictor does not promise a machine will not break. The RFQ estimator does not send a price until a person sets it.
- Brand orange on the main button is `rgb(240, 88, 40)`.
