## Rules held

- No catalog prices. The $497 AI Audit line is the price on the new pages. Industry cards say “Start with the $497 AI Audit.”
- No client stats or testimonials. Example days are labeled as examples. Catalog counts such as “12 tenants” or “in minutes” are not used as results.
- No “coming soon” lines.
- Texting pages say carrier approval is required and that texts are not sending from the site today. Email is named where it works without that step.
- Rent notices, renewals, showings, and application flags say a person reviews and decides. They say this is not legal advice. Application check and showing questions say nothing approves, denies, or screens a tenant on its own. Every prospect gets the same showing questions. Late-notice templates are ones the owner or their attorney approves, and a person approves the notice before it is sent.
- The TREC page says the agent and the broker confirm every date before it counts, and that it is a reminder tool, not legal advice.
- Listing drafts say the agent approves every word, and that fair housing and MLS rules still apply.
- Brand orange on the main button is `rgb(240, 88, 40)`.
