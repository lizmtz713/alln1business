## Rules held

- No catalog prices. The $497 AI Audit line is the price on the new pages.
- No client stats or testimonials. Example days are labeled as examples. The catalog note that fewer renewals mean fewer chargebacks is not stated as a result.
- No “coming soon” lines.
- Copy says we connect to or work alongside software. It does not claim a vendor partnership.
- The insurance claim page says the owner or the adjuster decides, and that there is no guarantee of a claim outcome.
- Text opt-out and membership renewal say texting needs carrier approval and is not sending from the site. Opt-out is a stop-list, not a new text program, and a lawyer reviews the rules. It is not legal advice.
- Warranty, paper posting, shelf counts, quality flags, ad changes, catalog specs, and reorders wait for a person. Quality inspection does not promise zero defects. Inventory forecast does not promise you will not run out.
- Brand orange on the main button is `rgb(240, 88, 40)`.
