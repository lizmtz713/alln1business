What is MCP, and why connect it to my business systems?

MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is an open standard that lets AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT connect to outside tools and data. With a connector in place, staff can ask about a customer, an order or a document and get an answer from your own systems instead of copying and pasting.

What the workflow can look like

  1. We list the systems worth connecting: CRM, files, spreadsheets, databases or your own app.
  2. We use an existing connector where one exists, or build a small one for your system.
  3. Access is limited: read-only by default, and only the data each role needs.
  4. Your team asks questions in ChatGPT or Claude and gets answers from current data.
  5. Any action that changes data, such as updating a record, asks for confirmation first.

Questions before you start

Is it safe to connect business data to AI?

It can be, with the right plan and settings. We use business accounts, limit access to what's needed and document what each connector can see.

Do I need a business plan for ChatGPT or Claude?

Connector features depend on the plan. We check yours in the AI Audit and recommend the least expensive option that works.

Can AI make changes in my systems?

Only if you allow it. Connectors are read-only by default and ask for confirmation before changes.

What if my software has no connector?

We can build one if your software has an API, or use Portal Runner if it doesn't.
