Who this package is for

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing and similar trade companies that pay for leads and run one or more crews, where calls come in while everyone is on a job and approved quotes go cold. A solo operator just going official can start with the launch stage; multi-crew operators usually start with the inquiry-to-paid system.

Problems we hear in this industry

The phone rings while you’re on a ladder

Missed calls during the day and after hours go to the competitor who answers first.

Quotes take too long and then go quiet

Estimates wait for the evening, and nobody has time to follow up on the ones that were sent.

Customer messaging eats the office day

Confirmations, “when is the tech coming?” and reschedules pile up across calls, texts and email.

Paid jobs don’t turn into reviews or repeat work

Review requests and maintenance reminders depend on someone remembering.

Job details get lost between the call and the truck

Address, photos and the actual problem don’t always reach the technician.

Fictional example: a three-truck HVAC company

Picture an imaginary three-truck HVAC company. A homeowner calls at 7:40 PM while the owner is at dinner. The call goes unanswered, so a text-back offers help within seconds (the homeowner had given texting consent), collects the address, the problem and two photos, and offers estimate times from the calendar. When the message mentions a burning smell, the system stops booking and alerts the owner by phone for a human decision. The next morning the owner reviews a draft quote, edits it and sends it himself; a follow-up reminder is scheduled for three days later.

Made up to show how the pieces connect. Not a real business, client story, testimonial or measured result.

How the connected flow works

  1. A missed call gets a text-back, or the AI receptionist answers and says it is AI.
  2. Address, issue and photos are collected; urgent hazards go straight to a person.
  3. The estimate is booked in your calendar or field-service tool.
  4. A quote draft (or Photo-to-Quote draft) waits for your edit and approval.
  5. Approved quotes get follow-up reminders; jobs get confirmations.
  6. After the invoice is paid, a review request goes to every customer the same way.

Illustrative design only. Exact steps, tools and approvals are agreed after your audit.

Questions before you start

Do I have to switch from Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Usually no. We design around the tools you already use. API access differs by plan and app type, so we confirm eligibility in the audit before promising a connection.

Will AI give customers prices?

Only what you approve. AI can prepare a quote draft from your price rules or photos, but a person reviews and sends it.

What happens with an emergency call?

You define what counts as urgent. Those calls and messages are routed to a person right away instead of being booked automatically.

Can it answer in Spanish?

Yes, bilingual English and Spanish flows can be scoped, with your approved wording in both languages.

Does the starting price include the phone number and software?

No. Phone numbers, texting fees, CRM and AI usage are billed by each vendor to your own accounts. The starting price covers our setup work for the agreed scope.
