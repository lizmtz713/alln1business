Where does the day actually go?

Owners do not lose the day to one dramatic problem. They lose it in five small leaks. The studies above describe the industry. They do not describe your shop until we look at your phone and your forms in the audit.

The call you cannot take. You are on the equipment. Invoca’s 2024 research put unanswered home-services calls at 27%, and almost nobody left a voicemail. The next company on the list gets the job.

The form that sits. Valve+Meter’s shoppers waited five days at 40% of the 466 companies. A form is a person who already picked you, then heard nothing.

The quote that never gets signed. You wrote the number in the driveway. It lives in a text thread. Nobody sent a clean copy, so the job starts on a handshake or not at all.

The empty driveway. The tech drives across town. The customer forgot. That hour is gone, and so is the fuel.

Sunday night with the books. Job costs, receipts, and “did we get paid?” wait until you are too tired to care. A person still has to decide what is real. The sorting does not have to be you.

How do I answer and win the lead?

The first conversation, by phone, text, chat, or a bought lead.

  • AI phone receptionist answers your line.
  • Missed-call text-back writes the caller who hung up. Carrier approval required.
  • After-hours dispatch wakes the on-call tech for the words you listed.
  • Website chat answers from your service list and hands off.
  • Lead follow-up keeps a form from going cold.
  • Marketplace lead replies answer Angi, Thumbtack, and similar leads you already buy.
  • WhatsApp for customers who already message you there.
  • Inbound lead research pulls public details before you call back.
  • Bilingual AI and Spanish for outgoing notes when the customer switches language.

How do I book, remind, and keep the customer?

The visit on the calendar, and the one they forget to rebook.

  • Booking reminders before the appointment.
  • Voice confirm and reschedule. The voice path is set up for you. It is not a line on this page.
  • No-show follow-up when the driveway is empty.
  • Cancellation waitlist so an opening does not sit unused.
  • Unfinished booking recovery when they started and stopped.
  • Maintenance-plan renewals for clubs and tune-up agreements.
  • Service-due reminders when the filter or the inspection is due.
  • After-job check-in once the truck has left.
  • Customer reactivation for people who have not called in a long time.

How do I quote, invoice, and get paid?

You set every price. Nothing is charged until you say so.

  • Quote and proposal drafts in your format.
  • Photo-to-quote when the picture should start the estimate.
  • E-sign and deposit before extra work.
  • Change orders when they say “while you’re here.”
  • Text-to-pay. The link is a text, so carrier approval comes first.
  • Collections for invoices that are late. A person decides the tone.
  • Failed payments when a card does not go through.

  • Local pages and AI search visibility so the facts on your site are easy to quote.
  • Content drafts from jobs you already did.

How do I keep the money straight?

A person reviews and decides before anything is paid, filed, or sent to a bookkeeper as final. This is not tax advice.

  • Job costing so the expensive job is visible before the books close.
  • Bills to pay and receipts.
  • Bank reconciliation and month-end close.
  • Cash forecast and budget versus actual.
  • Questions about your numbers, answered from your file, for you to check.
  • Sales tax watch and W-9 and 1099 readiness. Your CPA decides what is filed.

How do I get paperwork out of the truck?

The inbox, the glove box, and the portal that has no connection.

  • Invoice reading and paper and handwriting.
  • COI and license dates and permits and inspections.
  • Certificate requests from general contractors.
  • Insurance claim photo packets for storm work. You or the adjuster decides. No promise of a claim outcome.
  • Portal runner for sites with no clean connection, and data cleanup before a move.

How do I train the crew without repeating myself?

A person still hires, fires, and signs anything that changes pay.

  • SOP builder from how you already do the task.
  • New-hire onboarding and a coach on those procedures.
  • Handbook answers and safety talks in English and Spanish. Talks are not a substitute for required OSHA training.
  • Crew translator and voice daily logs.
  • Shift swaps. A person approves the change. Texts need carrier approval.
  • A safe-use policy so customer files are not pasted into a free AI box. A lawyer reviews the final policy.

How do I run the field?

The truck, the photos, and the parts.

Questions trades owners ask

Is this only for HVAC?

No. The same list fits plumbing, electrical, roofing, and cleaning. The words change. A roofer’s emergency is water in the house. An electrician’s is a burning smell. You write those words. Commercial builders can also start from construction and development.

Do I have to leave Jobber or Housecall Pro?

No. We connect to the schedule and the customer list you already pay for, or we work beside them. If the login will not allow a connection, we say that in the audit instead of pretending.

Will it text my customers on its own?

Not until you approve the words and the carrier approves the messaging. Texting is not sending from this website today. Email and the voice line do not wait on that carrier step.

Who sets the price?

You do. A draft quote can gather the address and the photos. The number is yours before anyone sees it. A deposit rule is also yours.
