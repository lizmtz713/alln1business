The problem

A carrier commission statement has to be checked against the policies the agency sold, and a missing line is easy to overlook.

What it does

  1. Take in each statement as a PDF or CSV.
  2. Read the policy, insured, transaction, premium, rate, and amount.
  3. Match each line to a policy in the agency system or the policy list.
  4. List missing, short, or unexpected commissions.
  5. A person decides every follow-up. Producer pay stays a draft until the owner approves it.

Illustrative steps only. This is not a client story or a promise of a result. Exact scope is agreed after the audit.

Independent insurance agencies.

Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft, or EZLynx, where each one allows access; carrier statement files; spreadsheets; email.
