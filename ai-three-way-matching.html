Who this is for

For businesses buying materials, inventory or job supplies where partial deliveries, changed prices and duplicate invoices create review work. This is a purchasing-control workflow, not another invoice reader: the third source is evidence of what was actually received.

Problems worth solving

Partial deliveries make total-only checks unreliable

A PO for ten units and an invoice for ten units do not prove that ten arrived. We can compare line quantities and mark short shipments or unmatched receiving records for the buyer to investigate.

A clean-looking match is not payment permission

Freight, tax, substitutions and unit-of-measure differences may need separate treatment. We propose a review packet that explains the mismatch and retains the documents; a person decides the resolution.

What a custom workflow could look like

  1. Collect the approved PO, supplier invoice and a staff-confirmed receiving record from permitted sources.
  2. Normalize vendor IDs, item references, units and document numbers without discarding the original values.
  3. Compare line quantities and agreed price/tolerance rules, separating freight and tax from merchandise where appropriate.
  4. Missing receipt evidence, duplicate invoice references and differences enter an exception queue; the system never invents confirmation of delivery.
  5. An authorized person reviews every proposed bill or change before posting. Payment stays outside this workflow; the decision and supporting evidence remain in a log.

Illustrative design only—not a client story, measured result or demonstration of a live integration.

Access and plan limitations

QuickBooks Online purchase orders are available in Plus and Advanced; we confirm company permissions and supported API fields before relying on them. Xero’s Accounting API includes purchase-order and invoice resources, with required OAuth scopes and developer-tier limits. Neither accounting platform should be assumed to contain a complete goods-received record. That third source may need an approved warehouse export or a staff-confirmed receiving checklist. If it is unavailable, we label the process two-way matching—not three-way matching.

Questions before you start

Is a receipt photo enough for three-way matching?

Not always. A sales receipt is not necessarily evidence that ordered goods were received. We identify a genuine receiving record or staff confirmation before describing the process as three-way matching.

Does a match automatically approve payment?

No. A match is supporting evidence only. A person approves the accounting entry and handles any payment separately.

Can we do this without purchase-order software?

We can assess an owner-approved PO register and receiving checklist as inputs. We will not claim a native accounting-system feature your plan does not include.

How is this different from AP Inbox-to-Bill?

AP intake extracts and prepares invoice data. Three-way matching adds comparisons against the approved order and receiving evidence before the reviewer decides what to post.
