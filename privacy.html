Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alln1 Business, 8236 Dover St, Houston, TX 77061 ("Alln1 Business", "we", "us"), collects and uses information through alln1business.com, our website chat, phone, text messages and services.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, business name, mobile number and anything you write in our contact form, website chat, email, call or text.
  • Call and text details: the number you call or text from, and when.
  • Website data: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting and chat providers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions, including texting you back after a missed call.
  • To schedule and deliver services you ask for.
  • To keep records of our conversations and work in our CRM.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure.

Text messages and your mobile number

We use your mobile number only to send the customer care messages described in our Terms of Service. You can reply STOP at any time to stop texts and HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. This excludes only the service providers that deliver our messages, such as our phone and messaging platform, which process your number solely to send and receive our texts.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM, phone and texting platform, email, scheduling and hosting providers, and only so they can provide those services to us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to work with you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing liz@alln1business.com.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
